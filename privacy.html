Privacy Policy for Zacky Blocks

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Developer: Mr ZACK

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zacky Blocks game handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Zacky Blocks does not require a login and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. We do not ask you to create an account.

2. Game Progress and Coins

Your coins, level progress, and game settings stay only on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

3. Advertising

Zacky Blocks shows ads provided by Google and its advertising partners, including AdMob. These advertising partners may collect device information, such as an advertising identifier, to show ads and to measure how ads perform, in line with their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy and Safety

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads shown in Zacky Blocks are intended to be family-friendly and are not personalised or behaviourally targeted at children. We do not track or profile children, and the game has no chat or social features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Apart from advertising partners described above, we do not share your information with other parties.

6. Third-Party Links and Services

The game may use third-party advertising services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them.

7. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected by us and game progress stays on your device, there is no personal account data for us to store or protect on a server.

8. In-Game Coins

In-game coins have no real-money value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be transferred outside the game.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Play Store listing for Zacky Blocks.
