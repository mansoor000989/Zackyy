Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Zacky Blocks game (developed by Mr ZACK) handles information. By playing this game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

This game does not ask for any account, login, name, email address, phone number or password, and it does not collect or store any personal information about you on any server.

  • Your game progress, coins and level unlocks stay only inside the game on your own device. They are never sent to us.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Advertising

This game shows ads (for example, a short ad you can watch to earn extra coins or extra moves). Ads are provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google (AdSense / AdMob).

  • Ad partners may use a device advertising identifier and basic, non-personal information (such as device type and general ad interaction) only to show and measure ads.
  • We never sell your personal information, because we do not collect any.
  • You can learn how Google uses data for ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Children’s Privacy & Safety

This game is a simple, family-friendly puzzle game and it is designed to be safe for children:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
  • Ads shown in this game are family-friendly. Ads are not personalised or behaviourally targeted at children, and no child’s activity is tracked or profiled for advertising.
  • The game contains no chat, no messaging, no social features and no way for strangers to contact a child.
  • The game contains no violence and no content that is harmful or disturbing for children.
  • In-game coins are earned only by playing and by watching ads. Coins have no real-money value.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided any personal information through an ad partner, please contact us and we will guide you on how to have it removed.

4. Third-Party Services

Apart from the advertising partners described above, this game does not share data with any third party and does not use analytics or tracking services of its own.

5. Data Security

Because the game does not collect or store your personal information, there is no personal data of yours on our side that could be lost or leaked.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when ads or features change. The updated policy will be shown on this same page with a new “Last updated” date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy or about your child’s safety in this game, you can contact the developer, Mr ZACK, through the app store page of this game.
